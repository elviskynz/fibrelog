Network records, always in your pocket

FibreLog is a record book for fibre network teams. Technicians save every FAT, FDT and closure in one place, so nobody has to rely on memory or paper notes.

  • Record the splitter, ports used and free ports
  • Save the exact location with a Google Maps link
  • Log the power level in dBm
  • Search and find boxes with free ports fast

Each company's records are private. Your admin creates your account, so log in with the details they gave you.
